05

MAKE IT SHORTER

A habit is easier to change when the cue is specific.

01

USE A SHORTER PLAYLIST

Pick 2 or 3 songs. Let the last song be your signal to finish.

02

USE ONE SONG AS A TIMER

Start a familiar track when you step in. When it ends, rinse and stop.

03

SHAMPOO WITH THE WATER OFF

Pause the flow while soaping or shampooing. Turn it back on only to rinse.

2 MIN SHORTER = 16 L LESS EACH SHOWER ≈ 4,992 L LESS PER YEAR*
* Example based on 8 L per minute and 6 showers per week.
